Privacy policy

Applies to verquoai.com and the VerquoAI apps for Android, Mac and iPhone. Last updated {{ updated }}.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address (to send sign-in codes) and your trading account ID.
  • Broker connection: the API key and secret you give us, stored encrypted, and the session your broker issues when you log in to it. We use them only to read your account and place the orders you or your strategies request.
  • Trading data: orders, positions, holdings and margins read from your broker, and the signals, orders and results the app produces for you, kept so the app can manage your positions and show your history.
  • Alerts: if you turn on alerts, the push address your browser or phone gives us for this device and the alert types you chose.
  • Usage: anonymous counts of pages opened and buttons pressed, under a random visitor ID in a cookie, to improve the app.
  • Access requests: the name, email, phone and details you enter in the request-access form.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.
  • We do not show your account or trading data to other users. The public demo uses simulated data only.
  • Alerts on your lock screen name the symbol and what happened; they never show amounts or account details.

Who else processes it

  • Your broker, through its official API, when you connect your account.
  • Push services (Google, Apple, Mozilla, Microsoft, depending on your device) carry alerts. Alerts are encrypted end to end; these services cannot read them.
  • Our email provider delivers sign-in codes and replies to access requests.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces the pages use.

Security

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS with HSTS). Sessions use secure, HTTP-only cookies, and every change you make is protected against cross-site requests.
  • Broker API secrets are encrypted at rest. Live trading, real orders and turning off the kill switch need a fresh confirmation code.
  • The app never stores your account pages, prices or orders on your device; it keeps only its own program files there.
  • Signing out removes alerts from that device. "Log out all devices" ends every session and removes every device's alerts.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, broker connection and trading history: while your account exists, or until you ask us to delete them.
  • Alert devices: until you turn alerts off, sign out on that device, or the device stops accepting them.
  • Usage counts: anonymous; the visitor cookie expires after a year.

Your choices

  • Turn alerts off at any time under Alerts in the terminal, or in your phone's settings.
  • Ask us to export or delete your data{% if contact_email %} by writing to {{ contact_email }}{% else %} through the contact form{% endif %}. Closing your account removes your sign-in and broker connection; on request we also delete your stored trading data and alert devices.
